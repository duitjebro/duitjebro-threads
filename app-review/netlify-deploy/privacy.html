Privacy Policy

Duit Je Bro Threads tool. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Who we are

Duit Je Bro is a Malaysian personal finance and takaful awareness account on Threads (@duit.jebro), run by Nik Arman. This policy covers the Duit Je Bro Threads app ("the app"), a private tool that manages the @duit.jebro Threads account through the Meta Threads API.

Who uses the app

Only the @duit.jebro account signs in to the app. Members of the public do not sign in to it or give it access to their accounts.

What the app does

  • Publishes posts and threads from @duit.jebro.
  • Reads replies to @duit.jebro posts and posts that mention @duit.jebro, and replies to them.
  • Searches public Threads posts by keyword (for example "takaful" or "medical card") to find people asking about takaful, medical cards, hibah or money problems, and replies to some of them from @duit.jebro.
  • Reads view, like and reply counts for @duit.jebro posts.

Information the app processes

  • Public post content from Threads: the text of a public post or reply, the author's public username, the post ID, link and time.
  • Statistics for @duit.jebro's own posts.

The app does not collect passwords, private messages, contact lists, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details from other Threads users.

How we use it

  • To decide whether a post is relevant and to write a reply to it.
  • To avoid replying to the same post or person too often.
  • To measure which @duit.jebro posts perform well.

Reply drafts are written with the help of an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) under fixed rules, and then published automatically from @duit.jebro.

What we store and for how long

We keep a private activity log of posts and replies made by @duit.jebro. Each entry holds the post ID, the username replied to, our reply text, the link and the time. The log is kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Search results that we do not reply to are not stored.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent any information. We share it only with the service providers that run the app:

  • Meta (the Threads API).
  • Anthropic (AI processing and scheduled runs).
  • Google (the private activity log in Google Sheets).
  • GitHub (the app's code, which holds no user data).

Your choices and data deletion

If you do not want @duit.jebro to reply to you, or you want your entries removed from our log, see Data deletion. You can also block or restrict @duit.jebro on Threads at any time.
