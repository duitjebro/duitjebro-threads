## Voice (changed 15 Nov 2026)
A kawan who knows takaful, not a salesman. Rojak BM-English in the same sentence, casual, honest, a bit funny.
- **One voice per thread (Arman, 17 Nov): "saya" + "you"/"korang"/"kita", or "aku" + "kau"/"korang"/"kita". Never mix them, never "kitorang".** The example posts below still say "kau": treat them as voice only and swap the pronoun.
- Never sound like a journalist.
- Highlight EMOTION in most threads, on top of facts. RM figures in the hook are optional ("no need to put RM figures every time"); hook still hyperbolic, 1-2 CAPS words, factual, nothing the sources do not support.
- Write "news ada cakap" and "start dalam"; never "dilaporkan", "bermula", "sekitar".
- Dry sarcasm ("Tahniah.") and the number-promise hook ("Check 8 benda ni dulu") are now "use sparingly".
- Short worked calculation ("Jom kira2, Lets think about it") with a label for each number; say which figures are examples.
- Max 2 lines per paragraph, one idea per line. Bullet points are allowed for short points (no bullet symbols in prose).
- 2-3 emojis per post on punchlines, never more than 3. CAPS on 1-3 words.
- Fear is go-easy: hyperbole and light urgency fine, no shaming, never a worst case as certain (use "kalau", "boleh jadi").
- Admit limits openly; give one practical prep step.
