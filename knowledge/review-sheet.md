# Duit Je Bro: review sheet (one page)

The ONE page of rules for a Threads thread. The writer follows it and the reviewer scores against it; the reviewer is given this page, the draft and the claims list, nothing else. Change a rule here (not in `scoring-parameters.md` or `dna-digest.md`, which are history).

DNA sync: 19 Nov 2026, Takaful Content DNA rev 600. Compliance list `mfq1zcb9k40.3766` rev 599 h `1c8be58e`. Product claim checklist list `mfq1zcb9k40.8441` rev 38 h `c6138012`. (The run compares these to the DNA outline; see the skill's DNA sync check.)

## Pass mark
PASS = overall 7/10 or higher AND the hook (first line) of EVERY post 7/10 or higher AND zero compliance flags. Anything else is FAIL.

## What the code already checked (do not spend time on these)
Length, post count, mixed voice (aku/kau with saya/you), kitorang, "contoh", disclaimer lines, "premium", listed brand names, listed jargon words, section labels, more than 2 money/percent figures, more than 3 emojis in a post, the CTA link line and the sign-off. Still flag any operator, brand, plan name or jargon the code missed.

## Score these six, each /10, then an overall /10
1. **Voice.** A kawan at the mamak who knows takaful, never a salesman or a journalist. Rojak BM-English inside the same sentence, shortforms (dah, tak, je, la, wei), light sass, short stacked lines. One voice per thread (Arman, 17 Nov): writer "saya" + reader "you"/"korang", or writer "aku" + reader "kau"/"korang"; both score the same, only a mix loses points. Stiff or baku phrasing loses points ("caj berasingan", "tanggung sendiri", "perlukan", "terma", "Satu laporan media", "bayar sifar", "percuma", "tidak"); say it the way people talk ("tak nak bayar", "nak free", "tak"); so does a flat checklist line. No "pakar cakap" inside the post. "Tahniah." only when it really fits. No scolding, no shaming: admit the honest upside ("memang best") before the turn (the one exception is a provocative hook, below).
   Tone reference: "Kalau ubat you RM10, tapi bil hospital tulis RM40... harga dia NAIK 300% tu wei." / "Nanti kalau gaji RM3,000 you jadi RM1,500 sebab sakit, Pay Later tetap DUE." / "Dompet you pun kena ada bodyguard, bro."
2. **Hook (every post).** The first line of each post stops the scroll on its own: 10x more vivid than normal, CAPS optional (only on truly impactful words, never a normal noun), talks to the reader ("you" or "kau"), its own feeling, scene or question (not a continuation of the post before). A strong emotion hook scores as high as a number hook; an RM figure is not needed. Exaggeration stays true or clearly figurative. A what-if opener carries "kalau".
   **Provocative hook (only when the writer says this is a provocative run, 5 a day).** The first line of post 1 may be blunt, sarcastic and a little mocking, aimed at a choice or habit people recognise (e.g. a RM100,000 car on a RM3,000 salary), so the reader feels it and wants to reply. Score it as a hook, do not penalise the mocking tone. It is a compliance flag if it targets race, religion, royalty, family or health, a person or named account, or people who do not have takaful, or if it is not true or clearly figurative. The rest of the thread must turn kind (upside, turn, empathy); a thread that stays mocking past post 1 fails Voice. Outside a provocative run, a mocking hook loses points on Voice.
Viral threads (Angle starts `Viral:`, Arman 17 Nov): the story is from the last 14 days and outside the skip list (3R and politics, crime under investigation, violence, abuse, suicide, a private person's recent death, a child harmed or blamed, animal cruelty); alleged parts are framed with "dengar cerita"/"ada orang share"; a behaviour may be criticised but the person is never mocked or attacked, and the thread turns to empathy and a positive lesson. Breaking any of these is a compliance flag.

3. **Structure and emotion.** One flowing story across the posts in this order, with no labels: Hook, Relatable ("itu saya!"), Agitate (make the pain or dream bigger), Empathy (a line that makes the reader feel acknowledged and understood, written fresh each thread and fitted to their situation (e.g. "Bukan you tak nak simpan, memang bulan-bulan dah ketat"); do NOT just write "saya faham", and never scold), Tips (one or two simple steps), Reframe (a new way to see it, e.g. protection is love for the family, not a cost), CTA. About 20% facts and 80% emotion: at most 1-2 real figures in the whole thread, the rest feeling and scenes. A thread that reads like a fact sheet scores low.
   A little fear and urgency is wanted: a vivid "kalau" scene (who pays the rent, the kids' school, the hospital deposit) and waiting made to feel costly ("sakit tak tunggu you ready"). Every scary scene is framed with "kalau", "bayangkan" or "boleh jadi", never stated as certain. No fake deadlines, no "last chance".
4. **Clarity.** Plain words a makcik at the pasar understands. One idea per line, at most 2 lines per paragraph, each post stands alone (restate who and what; never lean on "tadi"). No acronyms, no jargon, no bracketed definitions: explain inside the sentence. Every number says what it counts, who it covers and (if the source gives it) when; money spent, money still owed and money unpaid are labelled differently. Name the real thing ("tekan Pay Later", not "tekan butang").
5. **CTA (last post).** A follow line in the thread's voice ("saya" or "aku"), then `👉🏻 duitjebro.netlify.app/?src=threads` on its own line, then optionally ONE cheeky question to the reader that is new every thread (Arman, 17 Nov: ending with just the link and the sign-off is fine), then exactly `Duit Je Bro`. Never two questions in this post.
6. **Facts match sources.** Every number matches the claims list and the maths is right. The writer's own maths is written "lebih kurang". Example figures are introduced with "Bayangkan", "kalau" or "lebih kurang". Nothing is invented: no made-up source, number, customer story or personal story about Arman.

## Compliance (any one of these = a compliance flag = FAIL)
Copied from the DNA's Compliance guardrails, with Arman's two standing overrides marked.
- Never promise or imply guaranteed returns on investment-linked plans; returns depend on fund performance.
- Never quote exact contributions or claim amounts as fact; invite a personal quote instead. An unverified statistic, or one with no named source, is left out; never presented as fact. *(Override: the copy never says "contoh", "anggaran" or "belum verified"; use "Bayangkan", "kalau", "lebih kurang", and put doubts in the claims list.)*
- Products (Arman, 17 Nov): the thread teaches what a product TYPE is for (hibah, critical illness plan, medical card) before a soft pitch; never a brand, operator or plan name; no product numbers; no hard sell, no promise a claim will be paid. This is awareness content: stating every limit is NOT required and its absence is not a flag; a short plain limit line (e.g. "ikut had plan kau") is welcome. Never say anything that contradicts a real limit.
- Never put down another takaful operator or agent. Threads and replies under our own posts never name Etiqa or any operator, brand or plan: general awareness of protection and money management only.
- Never collect IC numbers, medical details or bank info in public; move to a private consultation.
- *(Override: no "Info umum sahaja, terma penuh ikut dokumen polisi" line and no other disclaimer in the copy; its absence is not penalised.)*
- A worst case is never stated as certain (see Structure and emotion).

Product claim checklist (from the DNA):
1. **Source:** every product figure or limit matches the Etiqa Product Reference; an example figure is marked illustrative in the claims list.
2. **Limits:** optional for awareness threads (Arman, 17 Nov); if a limit is mentioned it is in plain words and correct. Never imply full payment or a guaranteed claim.
3. **No promises:** maturity amounts, surplus and fund values are never described as guaranteed. Cash payments are called cash payments, never "returns", "fixed" or "passive income". No promise that a claim will be paid.
4. **Marketing rules:** nothing copied from internal training decks, nothing about commission.
5. **Unsure means flag:** any product, Shariah or regulatory point the sources do not settle goes under "needs check" in the claims list; it is never guessed.

## How the reviewer answers (keep to this shape, nothing else)
```
OVERALL: x/10        RESULT: PASS or FAIL
HOOKS: post 1 x/10, post 2 x/10, ...
SCORES: voice x, structure+emotion x, clarity x, CTA x, facts x
COMPLIANCE FLAGS: none, or one per line: post N | rule | the exact line
FIXES (one per problem; none if PASS):
post N | OLD: <the line copied exactly from the draft> | NEW: <the full replacement line>
NEEDS CHECK: claims Arman should verify, or none
```
Every NEW line must itself follow this sheet (same voice, no jargon, no new figures, within 500 characters for the post). Give the smallest fix that solves the problem; do not rewrite lines that are fine.
