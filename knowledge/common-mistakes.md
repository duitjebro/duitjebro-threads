# Common mistakes (writer checks these BEFORE the first review)

These are what the reviewer flagged most in the Bot Log (15-16 Nov 2026), plus the 16 Nov format rules. Go through every line after check-thread passes and before calling the reviewer. Update this list when the weekly report names new top fail reasons.

1. **Weak hook on posts 2, 3 and 4.** The most common fail. The first line of EVERY post needs its own punch: a feeling, a scene or a question with 1-3 CAPS words. Never open a middle post as a continuation ("Lepas tu...", "Sebab tu...", "Kenapa jadi macam tu?").
2. **A scary scene stated as certain.** "Family you AKAN hilang rumah" fails. Write "Kalau...", "Bayangkan...", "boleh jadi".
3. **Reads like a fact sheet.** More than 1-2 real figures, or a post that is only numbers. Swap a number for a scene: the anak, the sewa, the mak yang call.
4. **A number with no label.** Each figure says what it counts, who and when ("anak muda umur 30 tahun dan ke bawah", "tahun 2019"). Never put two figures of different kinds side by side.
5. **Old or headline-only figures.** Read the article; if the figure is old, say the year plainly or drop it.
6. **Own maths written as a fact.** Write "lebih kurang".
7. **Flat list lines.** A stack of bare items ("Sewa RM800 / Makan RM700") reads like a checklist. Tell it as a sentence or a scene.
8. **A paragraph longer than 2 lines.** Split it.
9. **A post that leans on the one before.** No "tadi", no "macam saya cakap". Restate who and what in a few words.
10. **Journalist wording.** "Satu laporan media", "pakar cakap", "menurut". Say "news ada cakap" or drop the attribution (the source lives in the claims list).
11. **A product explained with no limit, or with jargon.** Say the limit in plain words in one line ("kena tunggu beberapa bulan dulu baru boleh claim"), never as a disclaimer.
12. **Two questions in the last post.** Only one, the cheeky one.
13. **Scolding or shaming.** Admit the upside first ("memang best"), then the turn. Show you understand their situation in a fresh line (not a stock "saya faham"), never "you patut malu".
14. **An invented person.** No "kawan saya", "client saya", "ada sorang makcik" unless it is in the source. Use "Bayangkan".
15. **The wrong emoji on a heavy topic.** No 💀 or 😂 on death, illness or a family's loss; use 😬, 🥺 or none.
