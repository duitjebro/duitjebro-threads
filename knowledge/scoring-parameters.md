# Duit Je Bro: Draft Scoring Parameters (updated 2 Nov 2026; synced to the DNA's 15 Nov 2026 edits on 15 Nov)

> **History only since 16 Nov 2026.** Thread runs no longer read this file. The rules the writer and reviewer use are in `knowledge/review-sheet.md`; change them there.

Owner: Arman. Reviewer (Sonnet) scores every draft. Each parameter /10, then an overall /10.
**Pass mark: 7/10 overall.** Below 7: revise and review again until 7 or higher. Drafts always return with the score.
**Hook rule (changed 16 Nov, was 8): the hook of EVERY post in a thread (the first line of each post) must score at least 7/10.** Any hook under 7 is revised and reviewed again, even if the overall score passes.
**Re-score rule (added 31 Oct): every revision is scored again by the Sonnet reviewer before it goes to Arman, even a small tweak (one line, one word, one emoji). No unscored changes. Each message to Arman states the score of the exact text in it. If Arman asks for a small edit, make it, re-score it, then send it.** When Arman supplies his own wording, keep it and fix only what the reviewer flags, with the smallest change.
**Pronoun rule (DNA, 15 Nov): posts and replies say "you", "korang", "kita"; the writer says "saya" (Arman, 16 Nov), never "kitorang". Never "aku" or "kau".** A draft that uses "kau" or "aku" loses points on Voice match. Older examples below were written with "kau": keep their tone, swap the pronoun.

1. **Voice match.** Sounds like Malaysians in their 20s-30s posting on Instagram/Threads: group-chat friend, rojak BM-English, Malay shortforms (dah, tak, je, la, wei, confirm), light sass and banter, short stacked lines. Also matches the Content DNA example posts. Do not force very young slang (skibidi, 6-7). Never sounds like a journalist. Most threads carry EMOTION as well as facts (DNA, 15 Nov). Note: Instagram, Threads and TikTok block automated reading, so this style guide comes from news and marketing articles, not direct reading of posts.
   **Tone reference (Arman approved, 31 Oct; swap "kau" for "you"):** relaxed kawan-at-mamak, BM and English mixed inside the same sentence, e.g. "Kalau ubat kau RM10, tapi bil hospital tulis RM40... harga dia NAIK 300% tu wei." / "Medical card boleh tolong la, tapi ada had berapa ringgit dia bayar setahun." / "Duit caruman pun kena bayar on time, jangan skip." / "Dompet kau pun kena ada bodyguard, bro." **Added 2 Nov (Arman's own edits):** "Nanti kalau gaji RM3,000 kau jadi RM1,500 sebab sakit, Pay Later tetap DUE." / "Pay Later memang best, tapi bil dia datang pun on time. Kau bayar on time tak?" **Penalise stiff or baku BM and news-reader phrasing** (e.g. "caj berasingan", "tanggung sendiri", "perlukan", "dilaporkan", "terma"); prefer "caj asing", "kena ada", "news ada cakap". Do not penalise rojak English words. A checklist-style line (e.g. a flat list of terms) also counts as stiff. Dry sarcasm ("Tahniah.") is sparing.
2. **Hook.** The first line of each post is 10x more hyperbolic than normal ("I ate ramen" becomes "most delicious ramen I've had in 5 years"). Extreme, scroll-stopping, 1-3 CAPS words, talks to "you". A sharp RM/number contrast helps but is NOT required every time (DNA, 15 Nov); a strong emotion hook can score as high. Exaggeration must stay true or clearly figurative. **A what-if opener must carry "kalau" ("Nanti kalau ...", "Kalau ..."); a bare "Nanti ..." reads as a prediction (Content DNA: never present a worst case as certain) and scores lower (2 Nov).** Each post's hook must work on its own and uses its own number or angle. The "Check 8 benda ni dulu" number-promise hook is used sparingly.
3. **Clarity.** Simple, easy words, one idea per line, max 2 lines per paragraph, each post stands alone. Short bullet points are allowed for short points (DNA, 15 Nov).
   **Plain-words rule (Arman, 31 Oct): no acronyms and no jargon.** Say who or what in plain words instead of PAC (e.g. "orang Parlimen yang semak kira-kira duit negara"), markup ("harga dinaikkan"), itemised bill ("bil yang ada senarai satu-satu"), waiting period and exclusion ("kena tunggu beberapa bulan dulu baru boleh claim", "ada benda yang dia tak tanggung"). Explain everything as if talking to a regular man; no bracketed definitions, explain inside the sentence. Penalise Clarity for any acronym or unexplained jargon. Common words (medical card, bil, claim, RM) are fine.
   **Say what every number counts (Arman, 2 Nov).** The reviewer checks that a regular man can say, for each figure, what it counts and who it covers. Money SPENT ("dah BELANJA") must be labelled differently from money still OWED ("masih belum habis bayar") and from the part flagged unpaid; figures of different basis next to each other without a plain label are penalised (RM5.4 billion spent vs RM2.4 billion owed read as a mismatch). Groups are written in plain words ("anak muda umur 30 tahun dan ke bawah"); a bare "30 tahun ke bawah RM5.4 billion" reads as if 30-year-olds owe it. Do not guess a period or meaning the source does not give (flag it in the notes). Each post must restate who and what in a few words instead of leaning on "tadi" from an earlier post.
4. **CTA fit.** Every Threads CTA = follow-me line, then the tracked landing page link on its own line: `👉🏻 duitjebro.netlify.app/?src=threads` (tag added 1 Nov so the CRM records the platform; the plain link without `?src=threads` counts as a CTA error). Then the closing line(s), then "Duit Je Bro". Closing = one question plus a cheeky one-liner (new every post). **Arman (2 Nov) allows the cheeky line to carry the one question in his style (admit the upside, turn it, ask "you" directly); then there is no separate question line, so the CTA block has only ONE question.** The 👉🏻 counts toward the 3-emoji cap (per post).
   **TikTok scripts (on request):** the CTA says the link is in the bio, as a spoken line plus on-screen text (e.g. "Link kat bio, isi form 30 saat"); never a URL in the caption. Bio links: TikTok `duitjebro.netlify.app/?src=tiktok`, Instagram `?src=ig`, Facebook `?src=fb`.
5. **Compliance.** Per the Content DNA: no guaranteed returns or claim-approval promises, no exact figures as fact, unverified stats flagged or left out, product limits mentioned when a product is explained, no IC/medical/bank details collected, no competitor or agent bashing by name. **NEW (DNA, 15 Nov): never name Etiqa or any operator, brand or plan in posts or replies; the content is general awareness of protection and money management. A draft that names one fails Compliance.** Fear: a little fear and urgency is wanted (Arman, 16 Nov, replaces the 30 Oct "go easy"), as long as every scary scenario is framed with "kalau"/"bayangkan", never stated as certain, with no fake deadlines or invented stories. "Info umum sahaja" absence is noted but not penalised. The DNA asks for "contoh"/"anggaran" labels on figures; Arman's rule below overrides that in the post copy.
6. **Structure and emotion (Arman, 16 Nov).** The thread follows the 7-step flow Hook, Relatable, Agitate, Empathy, Tips, Reframe, CTA as one flowing story with no section labels. About 20% facts, 80% emotion: at most 1-2 real figures in the whole thread. A thread that reads like a fact sheet, stacks numbers, or uses finance jargon scores low here.
7. **Stats match sources.** Every number matches its source and the maths is right. The source article is read in full, not taken from a headline. Own calculations are written "lebih kurang" and listed as the writer's own maths.

Other copy preferences: no "pakar cakap" attributions inside the post (sources live in the claims list), keep maths light. **Never write the word "contoh" (or "contoh je" / "kiraan aku") in Threads copy (added 30 Oct).** Frame illustrative figures with "Bayangkan", "Jom kira2, Lets think about it", "kalau" or "lebih kurang"; the claims list still marks them as illustrative. **"Tahniah." is optional, not required (Arman, 31 Oct), and sparing since 15 Nov: use it only when the turn really fits.**
**Name the real thing (Arman, 2 Nov):** "tekan Pay Later", not "tekan butang". Write "Pay Later" as two words. **Honest upside before the turn:** admit what is good ("memang best") before the sting, end on a light direct question to "you", no scolding.
